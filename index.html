About Me

I'm a Computer Science undergraduate at COMSATS University Islamabad, Sahiwal Campus, who enjoys turning ideas into software that actually works beyond the demo.

My work sits at the intersection of full-stack development, mobile engineering, and applied AI. I build MERN applications, Flutter apps, REST APIs, and data-driven systems — and I enjoy going deeper into the intelligence behind them, from YOLO-based computer vision and OCR to classification models and emerging Generative AI workflows.

What interests me most is the engineering between the lines: an ERP where every role sees exactly what it needs, a mobile app that keeps working when the internet doesn't, a vision system that can detect and understand what a camera sees, or an AI feature that moves from a notebook into an actual application.

I'm currently exploring Generative AI, model APIs, retrieval, and AI-powered backend systems, with a focus on understanding not just how to use AI, but how to build useful software around it.

I like building things that look simple on the surface — and are anything but simple underneath.
